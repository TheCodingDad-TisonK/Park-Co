# Changelog

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-15): Mayor World Update

## NEW
- Teddy's Toy Tour: a cosy indoor ride for the little ones (level 20). Ride past a toy-block castle, a teddy tea party and a rainbow, under a moon and stars.
- A new loading screen with a dusk skyline, a little car rolling along a track, and a tip for the road.
- A brand new look for the main menu, HUD, pause menu, settings, panels and welcome card.
- The Log Flume has a second pool, a second lift through a rock tunnel and three long lanes winding home.
- The main coaster runs six cars (24 seats) with six station gates.
- Every worker, and you in third person, wears the Park Co uniform.

## IMPROVED
- Every coaster track has been laid again: longer, smoother and much gentler on riders. The Serpent, Baron's Drop, The Wild Hunt, the Dragon Ride, the Puffin Line and the main coaster all got a new layout.
- The Log Flume is much longer: 166 m a lap instead of 94.
- The Co Engine splash is shorter, so you reach the game faster.
- Fewer pop-ups: no more "Ticket sold" or "Riding..." messages, and repeats only show once.
- The Arena is quicker: shorter wait before the show, shorter boarding and a shorter show.
- The Skyline is easier to board: the way up to the platform is wider.
- The Skyline lift now tells you why it is shut until the first expansion.
- Night time: lanterns light up properly and there are far more glowing fireflies.
- Nicer timber on The Wild Hunt and the Dragon Ride, neater topiary, and the teacup rider sees over the cup.
- The Dragon Ride now stays inside the park, away from the railway.
- The station gates have their own STN GATES button, and each gate covers a whole car.

## FIXED
- Dives no longer twist the train round the track (the main coaster after the lift, and Baron's Drop).
- You are no longer stuck in a closed pocket after the Arena show.
- The doubled QUEUE sign is gone.
- Ride exits no longer drop you into a wall (Dragon Ride and Log Flume).
- Coaster platform ramps slope the right way.

## REMOVED
- The "Ticket sold", "Toy sold" and "N rode" messages.
- The old loading screen.

## 1.7.7 (2026-11-10): The North Run

The park's frame rate, the north end and the guests, in one pass.

## Performance
- **A much lighter frame.** The static bake used to hide the 60,000 meshes it had merged instead of taking them out of the
  scene, and every frame still walked all 81,000 of them. They are removed from the graph now, the painted props merge
  by colour into vertex-coloured batches, and a car's shell merges into a few draws while its lap bar stays its own part.
  In a view from the gate this is 6,446 draw calls down to about 3,500.
- **Quality levels that mean something.** The three sets used to differ only in resolution and shadow map, and changing
  them recompiled every material in the park (a freeze), so all three ran at the same rate. A level is a draw distance
  now: high draws to 150 m with light fog, medium to 100 m, low to 72 m with the fog closed in, and each brings its own
  shadow map and tree density. Small moving parts (carousel horses, dodgem cars, boats, gates) are skipped once the fog
  has them. Measured from the gate: 3,068 draws at high, 2,350 at medium, 1,916 at low.
- **Booth cameras** render four frames a second instead of one full pass every frame, which was the whole frame again.
- **Fewer trees**, and four crowns a tree instead of six.

## Fixed
- **The circle in the sky.** The sky dome was built 230 m out while the camera's far plane sat at 210 m, so the dome was
  never drawn: the "sky" was a flat background, and whenever the dome lagged the camera its near cap poked through as a
  hard-edged circle. The dome is pulled inside the far plane and follows the eye, so the sky is a real gradient again.
- **The flower clock and the hedge maze are gone** from the north gardens, as asked. The bandstand moved south to make
  room for the new ride's forecourt.
- **Guests arrive spread across the drive and the forecourt** instead of in one strip, and a spawn of twenty is put on a
  short queue rather than built, dressed and routed in a single frame.
- **Guest looks**: coordinated outfits from a palette instead of random primaries, with hats, glasses and a little
  variation in height.
- **The walk across the park**: guests now aim at a point just ahead on their path and round corners instead of stopping
  to pivot on every waypoint.

## Rides
- **The Wild Hunt** takes the place of Nocturne, The Wreck and Moonwood: one big outdoor coaster on the north strip,
  167 m of track, a 26 m crest, 65 km/h, six cars and twelve seats, with its own station, queue, booth and breaker.
- **The Log Flume** is a double-drop chute now: the chain lifts the log eleven metres, the long chute throws it into a
  rock gorge and its pool, the channel turns east through a boulder tunnel, climbs a second, shorter lift and drops
  again into a second basin beside the mill race, then runs home round the north end of the station. The station is a
  timber mill house with a slate roof, name board, lanterns and a bench, and the water wheel turns in the race.
- **Baron's Drop** is a double dive now: the vertical loop is gone, replaced by a second climb in the south-east corner
  and a steeper second drop into a splash basin under the fence, with the long banked run home past the gatehouse tower,
  battlements and braziers.

## 1.6.0 (2026-10-31): The Skyline Update

The park grows: an elevated metro across the whole park, and the first stage of the expansion.

## New
- **The Skyline:** an elevated railway the length of the park, 15 m up on a viaduct with smooth curves, green railings and lamps along the deck. You can walk the deck too.
- **Three stations:** Skyline North, Woods Halt and Skyline South, each with a brick clock tower, a lift up to the platform, a glass canopy, platform screen doors and benches.
- **The Skyline metro:** a modern three-car train in Park Co green and cream, with 32 seats, LED headlights and sliding doors.
- **No queue lane:** guests take the lift up at Woods Halt, wait on the platform and board at the doors. They ride to the end of the line and back, get off at Woods Halt and take the lift back down.
- **Ride it yourself:** take the lift up, aim at an open door and press E. You step off onto the platform at the end.
- **Drive it yourself:** climb into the cab at either end. Turn the key (K), pick a direction (R), power and brake with W and S, open and close the doors (O). L switches the headlights, H sounds the chime and Space is the emergency brake.
  - Stop near the platform and press O: the train creeps onto the stopping mark and opens up.
  - Take the key out and climb out (E) and the train goes back to running on its own.
- **Expansion:** commission a stage from the Expansion page on the office PC or the tablet. The land opens three nights later at 06:00, with a countdown on the tablet while it is being built.
- **A control cabin** for the Skyline at Skyline North.

## Fixed
- The train no longer judders through the curves.
- The station benches are wood now, not plain white.
- No more stone ball sticking up through the Woods Halt platform.
- Guests no longer dip through the floor when they step into the train.
- The train's doors no longer look open from inside.

## Good to know
Only Woods Halt is open for boarding for now. Skyline North and Skyline South are in land that later stages open, so the train calls there with its doors shut.

## 1.4.1 (2026-10-29): Improvement Update

A smoother park, a new set of carriages for the Lindenburg Railway, proper station gates at every ride, and a tidier way into the Lindenburg Arena.

## New
- **Stop assist on the railway:** when you drive the train into a station, hold Space and she brakes in and stops with every carriage door at its platform gate. The platform alignment screen in the cab offers it as you approach and shows when it is on.
- **New carriages:** all eight carriages of the Lindenburg Railway are rebuilt as proper heritage coaches, each telling its own tale. They have panelled sides lined in gold, a painted crest and a name scroll for the story they carry, turned corner posts, an arched roof with a painted ceiling inside, buttoned benches with armrests, leaf springs under the frame, and steps at the doors.
- **Station gates at every ride:** the Carousel, Teacups, Ferris wheel, Dodgems, Drop tower, Little Roundabout and Wobbly Pots now have proper railed gates at their entrance and exit, in the ride's own colours. The Log flume has a gate at each boat's berth along the dock. The gates stand open while the ride is waiting and close while it runs.

## Improved
- **Performance:** the park now draws about a third of the triangles it did. Rounded parts such as booth desks, buttons, trims and railings were built from far more geometry than they needed, which slowed every view of the park. They look the same and cost much less.
- **Performance:** the train is now a fraction of the work to draw. Each carriage, its wheels and its doors are built as a few solid pieces instead of hundreds of small ones.
- **Sitting on the train:** the driver now has a proper seat in the cab, and in 3rd person you see yourself sitting on it. In the carriages you sit properly on the bench, and the 3rd person camera stays under the roof instead of ending up inside it.

## Fixed
### The Lindenburg Railway
- The train now stops at the right place in every station. It used to stop about 19 metres too far, with the engine past the end of the platform and the carriage doors nowhere near the gates, both on AUTO and when you drove her yourself.

### The Lindenburg Arena
- AUTO now really runs the show by itself. With the key on, it opens the gates when guests are waiting, closes up once the line is in and seated (after at least half a minute) and starts the show. Before, it never closed the gates, so you had to open them and start every show yourself.
- The stair hall is now closed in at the top of the stair. You no longer see the sky through a gap between the hall and the stand's roof.
- The stone base along the front of the hall no longer runs across the doorway, so the red carpet leads straight in to the gates.
- The entrance's floating gold arch is replaced by a gilt door frame with a keystone, set properly round the doorway.
- The entrance gates are now proper railed iron gates instead of solid black panels.
- The cluttered model in front of the store door under the landing is gone, so the door stands clear.
- A velvet curtain now hangs in the way through from the stair hall onto the stand. It draws shut when the show starts and opens again when it ends.
- The stand's side walls now reach all the way up to its roof, so you can no longer see out over the stair hall's roof or into the stand from outside.
- Opening the gates no longer opens the curtain. The curtain now moves only with the show and the CURTAIN switch on the STAGE desk, so the city stays hidden while the audience comes in.
- The hydra now really grips the city: as it reaches the top, its forelegs come down on the gatehouse's battlements either side of the spire and its claws hook over the front edge. Before, its feet were buried inside the towers.
- The stage lights are gentler. The moving heads' beams are soft shafts instead of solid white cones, faint by day and stronger after dark, and the follow spot no longer burns out whoever it is on.

## 1.3.0 (2026-10-28): The Footplate Update

The Lindenburg No. 1 is now a real steam engine. The cab has been rebuilt from the ground up as a proper footplate, and you have to drive and fire her like the real thing.

The new footplate
- Rebuilt the whole cab around a real riveted boiler backhead with a brass builder's plate
- Added a firehole with sliding butterfly doors, and the fire glows brighter or darker depending on how she is burning
- Added two gauge glasses that show the water level in the boiler (the stripes behind the glass bend where the water is)
- Added a regulator handle on a toothed quadrant that swings up as you open it
- Added a steam manifold with valves for the two injectors, the blower and the air pump
- Added real gauges: boiler pressure (red line at 12 bar), steam chest, speed, and a two-needle air brake gauge
- Added a reverser bar on a notched quadrant next to the driver's seat
- Added an air brake valve with release, running, service and full positions
- Added cylinder drain cocks, a sander, an ashpan damper and a lubricator
- Added copper pipework around the backhead
- Added a shovel that swings from the floor into the firehole when you fire
- Moved the dynamo, doors, light, horn and passenger display controls to an electrics box on the right-hand wall
- Removed the old desk with the gas, brake and reverse levers and the four-dial panel

Driving a real steam engine
- Steam now comes from the coal you fire: open the firehole doors and fire a shovel at a time
- The blower and the exhaust draw the fire, and the damper sets how much air it gets
- Fresh coal smokes dark for a while, and too heavy a fire steams poorly until it burns through
- The injectors feed the boiler from the tender, but they only pick up above 3.5 bar
- Let the gauge glass run dry and the fusible plug melts: the fire goes out and she needs a service in the depot
- The safety valves lift and blow off steam when the pressure goes over the red line
- The engine will not move in mid gear: notch the reverser forward or back first
- Notching the reverser up at speed saves steam
- Cold cylinders fill with water: open the drain cocks until she is warm, or she knocks and wears
- Opening the regulator too hard from a standstill makes the wheels slip, and sanding the rail fixes it
- The air brake follows the handle with a short delay, and it cannot release without air in the reservoir
- The air pump runs on steam and thumps away while it fills the reservoir
- The cab light, headlamp and screens run off the dynamo and go dark without steam
- She starts in steam, standing in mid gear with the brake on
- Press N to let your fireman do the firing and the injectors for you
- On AUTO the hired crew now fires her, works the injectors and drain cocks, and notches up as she gets going

New keys on the footplate
- W / S regulator
- R / Shift + R reverser forward / back
- A / D brake valve
- G fire a shovel of coal
- V firehole doors
- J / Shift + J driver's / fireman's injector
- B blower
- K drain cocks
- X sand (hold)
- I light or drop the fire
- Y dynamo
- N fireman
- L cab light
- Every control can also be clicked

Cab fixes
- Moved the train HUD to the top left, so it no longer covers the controls
- The HUD now shows the regulator, cut-off, brake, boiler pressure, gauge glass, fire and tender
- The light switch now actually moves, and has its own lamp
- The first press of the light switch at night now turns the light off instead of doing nothing
- The STOP sign under the alignment screen is now fixed to the screen frame instead of floating
- The passenger screen text is bigger and sharper, so you can read it from the driver's seat
- The approach bar on the alignment screen no longer runs through the STOP POSITION text
- The alignment screen now shows an approach bar with the distance to the stop

Other train changes
- The brake now glides on and off with A and D
- Trains now wait 30 seconds at a station instead of 2 minutes
- Moved the train's stopping point at the stations
