# Changelog

## 1.4.0 (2026-10-28): The Booths, Arena and World Update

Every ride gets its own control desk, the Lindenburg Arena is rebuilt from the gate to the backstage, and the world now reaches far beyond the park.

## New

### A control desk for every ride
- All 18 ride booths, kids' rides included, have a desk designed for that ride, with its own colours, crest and Lindenburg Ride Works maker's plate.
- Each desk has a live display of its ride: coaster and flume track with the cars moving and block lamps turning red, the carousel and teacup seats turning and lighting up when taken, the Ferris wheel going round, the dodgems on the floor, The Drop's carriage, and the Haunted Manor's rooms lighting up as the party goes through.
- Live readings such as speed, height, G-force, rpm and wind. Aim at the display to see them.
- Ready lamps for key, gates, bars and riders, plus a READY lamp that lights when the ride can go.
- A sound button for each ride (band organ, foghorn, a roar and more), and a VOICE switch that plays it on every dispatch.
- Kids' desks have a toy look, chunky buttons and a big green GO! button.

### The world beyond the park
- The world now reaches about 620 m in every direction, and you can see much further.
- A construction site surrounds the park outside the railway: forest being cleared, earthworks with diggers and dump trucks, buildings going up under tower cranes, and the steel of a new ride. A viewing platform at the end of Windmill Halt's footpath looks out over the works.
- A new railway viaduct is being built across the park, in every stage from formwork to laid track. It bridges over the Lindenburg Railway and never joins it.
- The town of Lindenburg to the west, with a church spire you can see from the park, farms to the east, wind turbines, a lake south of Lakeside Halt, forest and hills on the horizon.

## Improved

### The Coaster
- New operator desk with a display of the train and station: every seat, every restraint bar, each platform gate and the air system.
- The wall track display is redrawn in twice the detail, with track blocks, the moving lift chain, a speed trail behind the train, live numbers and this lap's speed traced over the height profile.

### The Lindenburg Arena
- Guests now enter through a stair hall and climb to the top of the stand, so nobody sees the city before the show starts. The queue has moved to the new gates.
- A castle front with towers, banners and the arena's name in gold, and seating in the five riders' colours.
- Moving lights that follow the riders, and an effect for each of the five gifts: fire cannons, water jets in the moat, wind and fog, rocks rising from the sand, and a blinding light. Fireworks over the city at the finale.
- New music cues through the show.
- The hydra is remodelled: scaled body, claws gripping the city wall, wings, five necks with horned heads and glowing eyes. It now rises higher and leans out toward the audience.
- A full backstage area: stables with saddled horses, the hydra's lift pit and power unit, cast and wardrobe cabins, the fire-effects gas store and the moat's pump house.
- The control room has restyled desks, a running order of the 13 scenes, a lighting and sound mixer whose faders follow the show, and a PA microphone for your own announcements.

## 1.3.0 (2026-10-28): The Footplate Update

The Lindenburg No. 1 is now a real steam engine. The cab has been rebuilt from the ground up as a proper footplate, and you have to drive and fire her like the real thing.

The new footplate
- Rebuilt the whole cab around a real riveted boiler backhead with a brass builder's plate
- Added a firehole with sliding butterfly doors, and the fire glows brighter or darker depending on how she is burning
- Added two gauge glasses that show the water level in the boiler (the stripes behind the glass bend where the water is)
- Added a regulator handle on a toothed quadrant that swings up as you open it
- Added a steam manifold with valves for the two injectors, the blower and the air pump
- Added real gauges: boiler pressure (red line at 12 bar), steam chest, speed, and a two-needle air brake gauge
- Added a reverser bar on a notched quadrant next to the driver's seat
- Added an air brake valve with release, running, service and full positions
- Added cylinder drain cocks, a sander, an ashpan damper and a lubricator
- Added copper pipework around the backhead
- Added a shovel that swings from the floor into the firehole when you fire
- Moved the dynamo, doors, light, horn and passenger display controls to an electrics box on the right-hand wall
- Removed the old desk with the gas, brake and reverse levers and the four-dial panel

Driving a real steam engine
- Steam now comes from the coal you fire: open the firehole doors and fire a shovel at a time
- The blower and the exhaust draw the fire, and the damper sets how much air it gets
- Fresh coal smokes dark for a while, and too heavy a fire steams poorly until it burns through
- The injectors feed the boiler from the tender, but they only pick up above 3.5 bar
- Let the gauge glass run dry and the fusible plug melts: the fire goes out and she needs a service in the depot
- The safety valves lift and blow off steam when the pressure goes over the red line
- The engine will not move in mid gear: notch the reverser forward or back first
- Notching the reverser up at speed saves steam
- Cold cylinders fill with water: open the drain cocks until she is warm, or she knocks and wears
- Opening the regulator too hard from a standstill makes the wheels slip, and sanding the rail fixes it
- The air brake follows the handle with a short delay, and it cannot release without air in the reservoir
- The air pump runs on steam and thumps away while it fills the reservoir
- The cab light, headlamp and screens run off the dynamo and go dark without steam
- She starts in steam, standing in mid gear with the brake on
- Press N to let your fireman do the firing and the injectors for you
- On AUTO the hired crew now fires her, works the injectors and drain cocks, and notches up as she gets going

New keys on the footplate
- W / S regulator
- R / Shift + R reverser forward / back
- A / D brake valve
- G fire a shovel of coal
- V firehole doors
- J / Shift + J driver's / fireman's injector
- B blower
- K drain cocks
- X sand (hold)
- I light or drop the fire
- Y dynamo
- N fireman
- L cab light
- Every control can also be clicked

Cab fixes
- Moved the train HUD to the top left, so it no longer covers the controls
- The HUD now shows the regulator, cut-off, brake, boiler pressure, gauge glass, fire and tender
- The light switch now actually moves, and has its own lamp
- The first press of the light switch at night now turns the light off instead of doing nothing
- The STOP sign under the alignment screen is now fixed to the screen frame instead of floating
- The passenger screen text is bigger and sharper, so you can read it from the driver's seat
- The approach bar on the alignment screen no longer runs through the STOP POSITION text
- The alignment screen now shows an approach bar with the distance to the stop

Other train changes
- The brake now glides on and off with A and D
- Trains now wait 30 seconds at a station instead of 2 minutes
- Moved the train's stopping point at the stations

## 1.0.15 (2026-10-25): Make the use button a setting: E key or left mouse button

Use button is now a setting

You can now choose how you interact with the park. Pick the E key or
the left mouse button, whichever feels better.

- Find it under Settings > Use button, or press F6 at any time to
  switch.
- Left mouse button stays the default, so nothing changes unless you
  want it to.
- Your choice is remembered between sessions.
- On-screen prompts follow your choice, and the guide now refers to
  "E or left-click" at the ticket window.
