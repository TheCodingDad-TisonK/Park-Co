# Droomvlucht - design and build plan (a new Marerijk land)

**Version 1, drafted 2026-10-29. Awaiting tison's sign-off. Once signed, this plan is binding.**

This is the complete plan for adding **Droomvlucht** ("Dream Flight"), Efteling's suspended
dark ride, to Park Co as ride 21, in a brand-new **Marerijk** ("Story Realm") land. Unlike
Danse Macabre (which replaced the Haunted Manor), Droomvlucht has no Park Co analogue to swap,
so it is a new ride and a new land.

Where this plan does not say something, the agent does not invent it. It stops and asks tison,
and the answer is written back into this file before building.

---

## 0. The real attraction (research)

Droomvlucht opened at Efteling on 8 April 1993 (ceremony 10 June 1993) in the **Marerijk**
(Story Realm) area, designed by Ton van de Ven, with music by Ruud Bos. It is a **suspended
dark ride**: guests sit in small gondolas that hang from an overhead track, so they genuinely
"fly" through a dream world.

**Fixed facts from the sources:**

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Area | Marerijk (Story Realm), not Reizenrijk |
| Type | Suspended dark ride (hanging gondolas on an overhead track) |
| Manufacturer | Translift (+Siemens) gondolas, BHS Muenchen track/columns (originally CPM, who went bankrupt) |
| Track | 425 m |
| Vehicles | 28 gondolas, 2 seats each (travel in pairs) |
| Duration | about 6 minutes |
| Max speed | 18 km/h (only in the Zompenwoud spiral descent) |
| Track height | 12.5-13 m; building about 17 m |
| Height restriction | 100 cm |
| Cost | about 25.5 million guilders (about 12.5 million euro) |
| Figures | 73 (48 fairies, 10 trolls, 38 animals per one breakdown) |

**The story.** There is deliberately no linear narrative: it is the "logic of a dream", a
sequence of loosely-connected images that nobody can retell coherently. The dark tunnels
between scenes are the gaps between dreams. It draws on Germanic/Norse folklore: elves, fauns,
forest spirits, trolls and unicorns, ending with King Oberon (the elf king) on his sunflower
throne.

**The scene order (for the homage):**

1. **Kastelenrijk** (Castle Realm) - four swaying castles on rocks, waterfalls and mist,
   windows lit, a day-night cycle, moist-vegetation scent.
2. **Wonderwoud** (Wondrous Forest) - an elf garlands a bear, a flying elf, dancing forest
   spirits, a babbling tree, a unicorn.
3. **Elfentuin** (Fairy Garden) - a pond with elves, deer, a peacock, King Oberon waving from
   his giant sunflower throne.
4. **Sterrentunnel** (Star Tunnel) - a twinkling connecting tunnel.
5. **Hemelburchten** (Heavenly Strongholds) - floating, internally-lit castles and planets,
   shooting comets.
6. **Zompenwoud** (Squelch Forest) - the climax: a spiral descent at speed, a swinging troll,
   rain through the canopy, a startle troll.
7. **Droommoeras** (Dream Swamp / wake-up) - rabbits, a troll lifting three singing elves, a
   fog wall flanked by blue lights, the dream ends.

**Signature features:** the hanging "flying" gondolas; scent effects; glowing fairies and
Pepper's-Ghost effects; twinkling light; variable speed per scene; the fog "wake-up" ending.

**Operator room:** guests glide straight past the control room on the left at dispatch; the
control booth sits in/next to the load station. Interior layout is not publicly documented.

## 1. What this adds to Park Co

- A new land **Marerijk** (story realm): a themed plaza, a route in from the existing park, and
  the Droomvlucht show building.
- A new ride **Droomvlucht** (id `droom`), ride 21. The park moves from 20 to 21 rides.

**Decisions signed by tison (2026-10-29):**

- **Placement:** a new Marerijk land of its own.
- **Ride system:** suspended gondolas on a loop, Park-Co scale: about 6 two-seat gondolas on an
  overhead loop through all 7 scenes, slow drift with a faster swamp descent.
- **Operator room:** an indoor glazed control cabin at the station, the gondolas glide past it
  on dispatch.

---

## 2. Placement and footprint

**Proposal (flag for tison):** Marerijk in the south-west of the park, footprint
`x -72 to -34, z 45 to 72`, south of the Fairground and the kids' garden, west of the midway.
The Droomvlucht building sits at about (-53, 58), about 30 m wide by 16 m deep by 13 m tall.

- **Why here:** it is the park's story/fantasy corner (the Fairytale gate and the fairground are
  beside it), matching Efteling's Marerijk sitting near the park entrance.
- **Flag:** the car park currently occupies part of z 56 to 70 on this side. The land takes its
  west edge; the car park's western rows shrink or move. This is the one placement decision to
  confirm in-game (rule 2 of the expansion plan: clearance first; do not shift it on your own).
- **Route in:** a short spur south from the fairground/kids' lane at z 44 down to the land's
  plaza, in Marerijk's own pavers, with a Marerijk land arch.

---

## 3. The outside

The show building front is a large white facade with a central gate portal, two big flower
motifs (roses/tulips) framing it, and three towers/turrets above. A pond and a short bridge
lead to the gate. The back and sides are stucco with an arch frieze and a soft-glowing round
turret.

In Park Co this is one stone-cream building with a gabled front, two flower medallions either
side of the entrance, a central pointed portal, three small turrets along the roofline, a pond
with a bridge in front, and the sign **DROOMVLUCHT** in Marerijk colours.

---

## 4. The queue

- A tall entrance hall with white light-globes and climbing plants (a few globes sway).
- A partly-covered outdoor switchback, same ceiling treatment, troll-head door knockers on the
  heavy doors.
- The switchback ends at the load station, where guests step onto a moving walkway and into a
  passing pair of gondolas. The station hall also holds the exit, the souvenir shop
  (Dromerijen), a food point (De Gulden Gaarde) and the control room.

In Park Co this is the standard switchback lane (part 06) themed with planters and light-globes,
feeding a station inside the building where guests board. Guests never see the scenes from the
queue (rule 4 of the expansion plan).

---

## 5. The ride itself (the suspended gondola loop)

The ride system maps onto the **log flume's** moving-boat pattern (part 05): a closed course,
a few vehicles eased along it once per cycle, a station, gates and lap bars, with the scenes as
static sets along the course. The difference is the vehicles are **gondolas hanging below an
overhead rail** instead of boats in a trough.

- **Course:** a gentle loop about 90 m long through a long building, laid out as the flume's
  points are, with a slight rise over the swamp then the faster spiral descent.
- **Vehicles:** 6 gondolas, 2 seats each (12 seats total). Each gondola is a basket hanging
  about 1.8 m below the rail on a hanger.
- **Scenes along the course, in order:** the seven from section 0, as static sets plus glow
  figures, laid round the loop, each announced by a name board and its own light colour:
  Castles (pastel), Forest (green), Fairy Garden (flower pink), Star Tunnel (twinkle), Heavenly
  Strongholds (deep blue), Swamp (mud green, the faster descent), Wake-up (mist blue).
- **Speed:** slow drift (about 2.5 m/s) through the scenes, about 5 m/s in the swamp descent,
  eased like the flume's boats.
- **Restraints:** a lap bar per gondola (per-boat bars, like the flume).
- **Gates:** the station's boarding gates, like the flume's dock gates.

---

## 6. The operator room

A glazed control cabin in the station hall, built on the part 16 cabin kit (`pkCabinBuild`),
as Danse Macabre's is. The gondolas glide past it on dispatch. It carries the ride's own panel
sheet, and the queue can glimpse it from the station approach.

**Panel sheet (`PK_PANEL.droom`):** a new `droom` style (cream and sky blue, a music-staff and
star motif), a new crest (a hanging gondola under a crescent), and a new `flight` mimic (six
gondolas as pips on the loop, lit where a rider sits, with a height readout for the swamp
descent). `g2` reads altitude in metres; the voice button plays a music-box lullaby
(`pkDroomChime`).
