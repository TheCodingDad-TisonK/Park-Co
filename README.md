## What it is
A themepark with some "Efteling" vibes added to it. 20 rides + a train that rides in a circle.
All rides can be set either on AUTO mode where the ride does everything byitself. Or MANUAL mode where you need to control every part of the ride.
Some nice features like 3rd person camera (c), dev menu (f4) and even Fly Mode (F3) to get around the park quickly.

There is a office building with at the top your office, where a PC will let you manage the park or hire workers for you (10 in total).
You can also use TAB to open your tablet (wich basicly does the same when behind the PC).

Sell tickets, keep the queues moving and earn XP. Every level opens something new. A game hour is 75 real seconds, and the gate is yours to open and close.

## Player Movement
- WASD (walk)
- SPACE (jump)
- E (interact, also to ride the rides yourself)
- C (3rd person)
- TAB (tablet)
- F3 (Fly Mode)
- F4 (dev menu)

## Control Panel
Park co has 3 control groups; Ride Control, Park Control and on each station a Train Control

[Park Control]
- Open/Close park
- Circuit breaker with all 20 switches
- Park Lights
- Ride Lights
- Prices, hire/fire staff, service the coaster

[Ride Control]
Every ride has its own operator booth with:
- Operator key, Gates, Restraints, Dispatch
- E-stop, Test run, AUTO / MANUAL
- Maintenance key (RUN / MAINT)

On MANUAL the order matters: gates open, guests sit, gates close, restraints down, dispatch.

[Train Control]
- Every station on the Lindenburg Railway has its own Train Control
- Narrow-gauge steam train around the park, 5 stations and 3 level crossings
