## About

Step into the shoes of a park operator and bring your very own theme park to life. Inspired by the charm of **Efteling**, *Park Co* puts you in full control of running a bustling park from the ground up.

Manage your rides, run the narrow-gauge railway, keep queues moving, hire staff, and watch your park grow. Earn XP with every guest you serve, level up, and unlock more as your park flourishes. The gate is yours to open and close — how well you run it is up to you.

A full in-game day runs at **75 real seconds per game hour**.

## Features

- **🎢 20 Rides** – Run your attractions in **AUTO** mode, or take full control in **MANUAL** mode.
- **🚂 Lindenburg Railway** – A narrow-gauge steam train that loops around the park with 5 stations and 3 level crossings.
- **🎛️ Deep Ride Control** – Every ride has its own operator booth with gates, restraints, dispatch, E-Stop, test runs, maintenance mode, and more.
- **⚡ Park Management** – Open/close the park, flip the circuit breaker, control park & ride lights, set ticket prices, hire/fire staff, and service attractions.
- **🚉 Train Operations** – Take control of the railway from any of its station control panels.
- **🏢 Office & Tablet** – Manage your park from your office PC at HQ, or pull up your tablet (`TAB`) from anywhere for quick access.
- **🌻 Lively Atmosphere** – Guests that wander, sit on benches, chat, and litter. Sweepers clean up after them. Explore gardens with a flower clock, bandstand, windmill, hedge maze, the Lindenburg Arena, golf cart, and even a bed to skip to 07:00.
- **🛠️ Creative Freedom** – Enter Build Mode from the pause menu to shape your park to your liking.
- **🧰 Handy Tools** – 3rd person camera (`C`), Fly Mode (`F3`), and a Dev Menu (`F4`) to get around with ease.

## How to Operate

### Park Control
From the Park Control panel, you can:
- Open or close the park gate
- Manage the circuit breaker (all 20 ride switches)
- Toggle Park & Ride Lights
- Adjust prices, hire/fire up to 10 staff, and service the coaster

### Ride Control
Each ride features its own operator booth with the following:
- Operator Key • Gates • Restraints • Dispatch
- E-Stop • Test Run • AUTO / MANUAL
- Maintenance Key (`RUN` / `MAINT`)

**On MANUAL:** The correct sequence matters — *Gates Open → Guests Board → Gates Close → Restraints Down → Dispatch*

### Train Control
Each station on the **Lindenburg Railway** has its own dedicated Train Control panel to operate the train around the loop.
